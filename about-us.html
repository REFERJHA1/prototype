    ABOUT US

    Over four decades of precision engineering excellence, delivering world-class CNC machined components to industries worldwide.

    Who We Are

    PRECISION ENGINEERING FOR CRITICAL INDUSTRIES

    UC Industries is a leader in precision CNC manufacturing with over 40 years of expertise. We specialize in delivering high-performance machined components that power the automotive, railway, and aerospace industries worldwide. Our commitment to excellence is reflected in every component we manufacture, backed by IATF 16949 certification and an unwavering dedication to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction.

    With two state-of-the-art facilities, 120+ skilled professionals, and 35+ advanced CNC machines, we combine cutting-edge technology with traditional craftsmanship. We understand that our clients depend on us for precision—which is why we maintain micron-level accuracy and 100% traceability on every batch. Our mission is not just to meet expectations but to exceed them, delivering components that perform flawlessly in the most demanding applications.

    Quality Management Systems

    We maintain IATF 16949:2016 and ISO 9001:2015 certified quality management systems that ensure consistent adherence to international standards across all manufacturing processes.

    In-house inspection capabilities with calibrated measurement equipment ensure dimensional accuracy and compliance with customer specifications at every stage of production.
